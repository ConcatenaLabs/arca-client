This wallet holds every Sequentia asset as Arca coins against an operator you choose, and BTC on Bitcoin. It reads the chain only from your own Sequentia Core node.

1 · Your Sequentia node

The wallet needs a node that keeps a transaction index (-txindex) and validates its Bitcoin anchors (-validateanchor): finality on Sequentia is the node's, and it follows Bitcoin.

2 · The operator

The wallet pins the operator's key when it joins and shows it: compare it with the key the operator publishes through a channel you trust.

Leaves and refresh

Each leaf in the ark expires; a refresh replaces leaves with new ones from the server's next round, at its refresh fee. The app has Bark refresh leaves near expiry on its own every half hour while it runs.

Exit · take every leaf to the chain alone

An exit needs nothing of the ark's server: the leaves' transactions go on the chain, then, after the exit delta, a claim brings the BTC to the on-chain wallet. Each step waits for blocks.

Board

Boarding brings on-chain coins into Arca. Pay the wallet's on-chain address first; the board is spendable once its transaction is final.
